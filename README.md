# hutch

**Your things, privately kept.**

Hutch is an iPhone app for keeping track of everything you own, end-to-end encrypted, so no one sees your inventory but you. When you choose, you open a small door: lend the ladder to a neighbor, the folding table for a party. And when it's time, sell something on eBay. It exists because [every app I looked at](docs/context.md) made you trade privacy for sharing.

Like the furniture it's named after: closed doors below, a few things on display. That idea runs through [the logo and the design](design/README.md), too.

Hutch is being built one milestone at a time. The [plan](docs/plan.md) lays out the road, [the milestones](https://github.com/chtitoon/hutch/milestones) show where things stand, and the [decisions](docs/decisions.md) record what's settled and why.
